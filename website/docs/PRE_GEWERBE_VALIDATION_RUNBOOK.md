# AmtHero24 Pre-Gewerbe Market Validation Runbook

Status: research-only pre-launch validation. This document does not authorize Closed Beta admission, public commercial operation, payments, or processing real administrative cases as an ongoing service.

## Objective

Test whether people understand and value the AmtHero24 proposition before the operational Closed Beta and before relying on the product as a commercial service.

The safest useful signal is not raw traffic. It is whether repeated voluntary research participants:

1. understand the product without a long explanation;
2. recognize a real administrative pain point;
3. trust the product boundaries;
4. identify one or more MVP journeys they would want to use after formal launch;
5. can explain what would stop them from trying it.

## What may be shown

Use only the reviewed noindex preview and synthetic material:

- landing page;
- six local 15-second product demos;
- synthetic before/after examples;
- screenshots;
- explanation of the six MVP journeys;
- privacy/safety explanation;
- the fact that operational Beta access is not yet open.

Do not ask participants to upload or forward their real letters, contracts, invoices, voice messages, appointments, identifiers, or other administrative documents during this research phase.

## Research script

Keep each conversation short and neutral. Do not sell or promise launch access.

### Opening

> Ich teste gerade, ob die Idee von AmtHero24 verständlich und hilfreich wirkt. Es ist noch kein öffentlicher Dienst und ich möchte heute keine persönlichen Unterlagen von dir. Schau dir bitte nur die Demo an und sag mir offen, was du verstehst und was nicht.

### Questions

Ask in this order:

1. **Was glaubst du nach dieser Seite, was AmtHero24 macht?**
2. **Welche dieser Situationen kennst du selbst: Briefe verstehen, Termine, Kündigung, Verträge, Geld zurück oder formelle Nachrichten?**
3. **Welche davon wäre für dich am wichtigsten? Warum?**
4. **Was macht dich bei so einem KI-Assistenten skeptisch oder unsicher?**
5. **Welche Information fehlt dir auf der Seite, bevor du so etwas später ausprobieren würdest?**
6. **Wenn der Dienst nach dem offiziellen Start kostenlos testbar wäre: würdest du ihn wahrscheinlich ausprobieren, vielleicht ausprobieren oder eher nicht? Warum?**
7. **Was würdest du an der Seite oder Erklärung zuerst ändern?**

Do not ask for a purchase, preorder, payment details, or real case processing.

## Observation sheet

Record aggregate/sanitized observations only. Do not put names, phone numbers, email addresses, document contents, or verbatim private stories in GitHub.

For each research session, record locally and later aggregate:

- participant number: sequential research ID only;
- language used;
- understood proposition without help: yes / partly / no;
- top journey selected: one of six / none;
- strongest trust concern category;
- strongest missing-information category;
- stated future trial intent: likely / maybe / unlikely;
- one sanitized usability observation.

## Decision framework

Do not invent a conversion KPI from a tiny sample. Use repeated patterns.

### Continue toward registration / operational Beta preparation when

- most conversations independently identify the intended problem;
- at least several participants can explain AmtHero24 correctly without coaching;
- one or more MVP journeys repeat as meaningful needs;
- trust objections are addressable by product/legal/UX changes rather than fundamental rejection;
- there is repeated voluntary intent to try after formal launch.

### Revise before registration when

- people consistently misunderstand the product;
- the six journeys feel too broad or confusing;
- the page creates more legal/privacy fear than confidence;
- participants want a materially different primary problem solved;
- the CTA/value proposition attracts interest for the wrong reason.

### Stop or rethink when

- repeated participants do not recognize the problem;
- willingness to try depends on capabilities the product deliberately will not provide;
- safe/legal product boundaries destroy the perceived value;
- the value proposition requires unsupported legal, financial, medical, or government-authority claims.

## Privacy boundary

This validation phase is deliberately designed to avoid a lead database and avoid processing real administrative cases.

- no real documents;
- no automated waitlist;
- no WhatsApp operational onboarding;
- no Hero Memory consent collection for research participants;
- no mission/reminder creation;
- no payments;
- no recording of participant PII in GitHub/CI;
- no public testimonials without a separate explicit permission process.

Market validation does not itself authorize Wave 1.

2. finalize the public Impressum/operator facts;
3. complete qualified German legal review;

5. provision and verify public contact email;
6. deploy and verify the website/domain/TLS configuration;
